═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  🚀 AFTER CREATING TEMPLATE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Click "Submit for approval"
2. Wait 1-3 business days for Meta approval
3. Check status: Twilio Console → Content Templates
4. After approval, copy the Content SID (starts with HX...)
5. Add to your .env file:

   TWILIO_CONTENT_SID=HXxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx

6. Restart your server
7. Test with: POST /api/invitations/test-whatsapp
